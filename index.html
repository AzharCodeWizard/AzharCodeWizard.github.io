01About

Careful engineering, calm design.

I'm a Lead Android Engineer with 6.5+ years of building production apps and SDKs in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. I enjoy the whole path from an idea to a release: architecture, code reviews, mentoring, performance and security.

Outside work I design, build and publish my own Android apps, from an offline Delhi Metro guide and a Quran and prayer times companion to a private on-device AI chat, and I write about what I learn on Medium.

I care about apps that start fast, stay stable and look considered, and I like tooling that makes the next migration or release easier.
